2. What the app is

A desk assistant for merchants who trade on Binance P2P. It replies in the chat of your own orders with templates you write, keeps a history of your own orders and exports it, and can adjust the price of your own published advertisements within the limits you set. It is not a wallet, not an exchange and not a trading bot.

The difference between the two versions is where it runs and where that history lives. In the web version, the one available today, the engine runs on servers we operate and your data — including your encrypted Binance API key — lives there too, which is why it keeps answering your order chat while your own computer is off. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what is stored, where, and what that design does not protect you from. In the Windows version, still in certification at the Microsoft Store, everything runs on your own PC and the history is a file on your own machine. You choose which one you use.
